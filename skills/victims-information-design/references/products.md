# Products

What to use for each kind of product. The website and the partner dashboard are real, built products. The other rows are derived from their rules and marked as such. When a derived rule meets a real case that contradicts it, follow the website.

## Website pages (the master)

- Build pages from the page furniture (**Header**, **Banner** or **HomeHero**, **Breadcrumbs**, **PageActions**, **Footer**, **QuickExit**) and the content blocks, in the site’s wrappers.
- Page types and their openings: homepage (**HomeHero**, then **Overview** with the wayfinder card, then **TwoColumnCards** and **CallToAction**); section landing (section **Banner** with callout title, **Overview**, **GroupedTiles**); content page (content **Banner**, **Breadcrumbs**, **Overview** lead, **TableOfContents**, blocks, **FeaturedLinks** previous and next); Get help now (**WayfinderTabs**).
- Every page keeps the topbar numbers and the quick exit.

## Partner publications: the A3 dashboard (built)

- A3 landscape (420 × 297mm), margins 10, 12 and 7.5mm. HTML is the primary edition and the PDF is the attached printable copy, made from the same file.
- A 12-unit measure with 9mm gutters (`pub-gutter`). Hairline `line` rules between modules and rows, newspaper-style, instead of boxes. At most one **EngagementPanel** per page.
- Lead page: **Masthead** with key numbers overlapping it, then a **FeatureChart** and supporting modules, a **StatusList** for work, and the **AccessibilityStrip** with the colophon. Continuation pages use a 17mm running head (logo, title, dateline) with the enlarged mark as a watermark.
- Type is the Publication group (`pub-*`). Body is 9pt and steps down to 8.6pt, then 8.2pt, for the whole page, never below 7pt.
- Charts: inline SVG, navy primary line, dashed `compare` comparison, direct labels, a screen-reader data table, no pies or gauges.
- On screen, sheets sit on the `desk` grey. They reflow to one column on phones and at high zoom.
- Synthetic editions carry the `sun` “Sample edition” badge and a colophon statement.

## Factsheets and A4 documents (derived)

- Take the publication rules at A4 portrait: masthead panel in `mist` with the logo, Merriweather titles, Fira Sans 10–11pt body in `ink`, and `teal-dark` for one lead paragraph.
- Use **Alert** styling (bordered, `radius-md`, icon top left) for the safety box. Every factsheet that may reach someone at risk carries 111 and 0800 650 654.
- Give file links the site’s pattern: “Title (PDF, 577 KB)”.

## Presentations (derived)

- Title slides use the hero-and-card pattern: a `mist` panel with the enlarged mark cropped off the edge, a white card with a Merriweather title.
- Content slides: white ground, `h2` Merriweather navy titles, Fira Sans text, one idea per slide, data styled as in the publications.
- The botanical illustration at most once per deck, decorative.

## Email and short updates (derived)

- Plain text first. Use navy headings, Fira Sans with a system sans fallback, and one call to action as an underlined link, never the yellow pill.
- Covering notes for partner editions point to the HTML first and the PDF for printing.

## Never, in any product

- Don’t use traffic-light colours for data about victimisation.
- Don’t use the `yellow` pill for anything but Get help now, or `sun` for decoration.
- Don’t use stock photography of distress.
- Don’t hide or delay emergency contact details behind a click.
