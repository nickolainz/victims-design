## Known gaps (from the system itself)

- **Not synced:** minor greys used only by legacy or third-party styles (`#ddd`, `#595956`, `#e9e9e9`, `#f5f5f5`, launch-button bevels), the unused `.btn--*` classes, the Shielded help widget and the search-overlay photo.
- **Video** has a guideline but no markup file, because the system could not embed YouTube.
- The site's 26 `@font-face` rules were removed from `bundle.css`; `tokens.css` declares the fonts instead (16 faces).
- `line` (1.5:1) and `control-line` (2.6:1) fall short of 3:1 in the source and are kept exact; never let either be the only boundary of a control.

## Re-syncing

1. Compare against the live site first; it is the master.
2. If the design-system artifact has been updated, re-export its `project/` files and repeat the mapping above (`assets/source/design-system.json` maps upload ids to names).
3. Edit `assets/tokens/tokens.json`, run `python3 scripts/build_tokens_css.py`, then `python3 scripts/verify.py`.
4. Update the dates and version at the top of this file.
