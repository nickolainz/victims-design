# AccessibilityStrip

The one-line accessibility and languages strip at the foot of a publication, followed by the colophon (numbered notes, team, contact and the sample statement).

**Strip** (`.strip`): a trio title, then items made of a status glyph, the format name in navy semibold, its status in teal (“In progress”, “Available”, “Planned”) and a muted note. Every status is written as a word as well as shown by the glyph.

**Colophon** (`.colophon`): a `line` rule over 7.5pt muted text. Notes are numbered in teal semibold and match the superscripts on key numbers. The team and contact sit right. On synthetic editions the colophon ends with “Sample edition: all figures and content are synthetic test data, not real results.”
