# Overview

The opening of a page: a short “Overview” in the section’s lead style. On the homepage and section pages it is paired with the **Get help now** wayfinder card.

**Variants**
- `moj-overview--with-wayfinder` (homepage, section landing): heading, one or two `p-large` paragraphs, and `.moj-overview__wayfinder`: a white-to-surface card (`radius-md`, `shadow-card`) with the `get-help-now` icon, an `h5` title, one line of `slate` copy and the yellow `moj-overview__wayfinder-action` pill.
- `block-content-page__overview` (content pages): an `h2.h3` “Overview” and the lead paragraph in `teal-dark`, `lead` style.

**The consumer provides** one or two plain sentences saying what the page covers and who it is for.

**Rules**
- The lead colour is `teal-dark` (6.1:1 on white). Never use it for body text below it.
- The yellow pill always reads “Get help now” and always goes to /get-help-now.
