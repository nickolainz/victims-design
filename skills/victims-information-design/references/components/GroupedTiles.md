# GroupedTiles

“In this section”: groups of link tiles on section landing pages, each group under a Merriweather heading.

**Markup**: `.block-grouped-tiles > .inner` with an `h2.block-grouped-tiles__title.h3`, then one `.block-grouped-tiles__tab` per group: an `h3.block-grouped-tiles__tab-title.h4` (Merriweather) and `.block-grouped-tiles__tiles`, a row of `a.block-grouped-tiles__link`. Each tile has a navy top border, `surface` ground, `shadow-card`, a bold Merriweather title with an arrow, and one sentence of `body-small` description.

**The consumer provides** group names and, per tile, the page title and a one-sentence description starting with a verb (“Find out how courts may help…”).
