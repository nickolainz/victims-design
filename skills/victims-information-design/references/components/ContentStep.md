# ContentStep

A numbered step: a large number in a circle beside the step’s heading and instructions, for procedures a reader follows in order.

**Markup**: `.content-element__content--step` with `.content-step` (the number, `radius-step` circle) and a `.typography` body holding an `h3.h4` and the instructions.

**Rules**: one block per step, numbered from 1. Keep each step to one action. This preview uses the green section theme, as on the Hide my visit and Privacy pages.
