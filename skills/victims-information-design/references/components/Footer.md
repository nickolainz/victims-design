# Footer

The site footer: a sitemap of every section and its children on a mist-to-white gradient, then copyright, the secondary links and the New Zealand Government logo.

**Structure** (`footer.footer`): `nav.footer__menu` lists each section as a bold navy `footer__menu-link` with an arrow, and its children beneath in Fira Sans 14px. `.footer__links-container` holds “© 2026 Copyright Victims Information” and the Accessibility, Privacy and Contact Us links. `.footer__logos` holds `assets/Logos/nz-government-logo.png`, linked to newzealand.govt.nz.

**Rules**
- The footer is the full sitemap. Its order is the site’s sibling order, so keep it in step with the navigation.
- The NZ Government logo is used as supplied, never recoloured.
- The live footer also hosts the third-party Shielded help widget and the quick exit bar. Both are left out of this preview.
