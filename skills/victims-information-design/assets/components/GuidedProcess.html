Guide me through the Coroners Court process

This section guides you through what may happen soon after a death is referred to the .

Every situation is different. The steps that happen next can depend on the circumstances of the death and what information the coroner needs to understand a death.

How to use this tool:

  • Press 'back' to go back to the Coroners Court page.
  • Press 'begin journey' to start. You will be asked to answer questions about the coronial process to find out what your journey might be like.
  • If you want to start your journey again, click the ‘reset’ button at any time.

Who can help

You may be unsure of what you need to do after a loved one dies. Te Hokinga ā Wairua End of Life Service can guide you, your family and whānau during this time. Te Hokinga ā Wairua can help you with things like arranging the funeral, registering the death and working through your loved one’s estate.
