Who to complain to

  1. For complaints about your experience as a victim

    The Chief Victims Advisor - Ruth Money, is an independent advisor to the Minister of Justice and the government on victim-survivor issues.

    She cannot act as an advocate or advisor for individual victims but is focused on amplifying the voice of victims to ensure improvements are made.

    Make a complaint to the Chief Victims Advisor

    If you would like to share your experience, highlight issues, or need advice on navigation, you can contact the Chief Victims Advisor by:

    Email: chiefvictimsadvisor@justice.govt.nz

    Sending a letter addressed to the Chief Victims Advisor to National Office, Ministry of Justice, SX10088, Wellington

    Find out more about the Chief Victims Advisor(external link)

  2. For complaints about a government agency

    You can complain to the Office of the Ombudsman if you think a government agency has treated you unfairly.

    The Office of the Ombudsman is independent from government agencies and offers a free service. The Ombudsman cannot investigate court decisions.

    Make a complaint to the Ombudsman

    Before contacting the Office of the Ombudsman, you will usually need to make a complaint to the government agency first.

    Find more information about a government agency, and how to contact them

    If you are not happy with the agency’s response, you can make a complaint to the Office of the Ombudsman.

    You can make a complaint to the Ombudsman by:

    • Filling in their online complaint form(external link)
    • Sending a letter addressed to the Office of the Ombudsman, Freepost, PO BOX 10152, Wellington 6140

    More information about what you can complain to the Office of the Ombudsman about can be found on their website.

    Make a complaint | Office of the Ombudsman(external link)

  3. For complaints about police officers, staff, or services.

    You can contact the New Zealand Police, or you can make a complaint to the Independent Police Authority (IPCA), who is independent from the police.

    Make a complaint to the police

    You can make a complaint to the police by:

    • Filling out their online complaint form(external link)
    • Calling 105
    • Visiting your local police station
    • Sending a letter addressed to the Police Commissioner, Police National Headquarters, PO Box 3017, Wellington 6140.
    • For a less formal option, you can fill in the police’s online feedback form(external link)

    Make a complaint to the IPCA

    You can make a complaint to the IPCA by:

    • Filling out their online complaint form(external link)
    • Emailing them at info@ipca.govt.nz
    • Sending a letter addressed to IPCA, PO Box 25221, Wellington 6140.
