If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call the Police on 111

If you need the Police but can’t speak for fear of harm, you will be asked to press keys for emergency assistance. If hearing or speaking is difficult, you can sign up to 111 TXT

If you need to leave this website quickly, use the ‘quick exit button’ at the bottom of the screen.

Who can help

You may be unsure of what you need to do after a loved one dies. Te Hokinga ā Wairua End of Life Service can guide you, your family and whānau during this time. Te Hokinga ā Wairua can help you with things like arranging the funeral, registering the death and working through your loved one’s estate.
